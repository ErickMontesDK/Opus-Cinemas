About Us

Where Code Meets the Silver Screen

Founded in 2025 by a passionate collective of students from the Vancouver-based Canadian College of Technology and Business (CCTB), Opus Cinemas represents the perfect blend of technological innovation and cinematic artistry.

What started as a final project for the Internet Programming course quickly evolved into a vision: to create a seamless, student-driven platform that makes the magic of cinema accessible to everyone in Vancouver, from the West End to the North Shore.

How We're Different

Smart Database Backbone

Our Supabase-powered system securely stores seat bookings and payment details, ensuring real-time accuracy without users ever seeing the complexity.

Dynamic Movie Carousel

Know exactly what's playing now or coming soon through our auto-updating display, powered by live API integrations.

Visual Seat Selection

Our interactive auditorium map lets you claim your perfect spot with one click, with instant reservation updates.
